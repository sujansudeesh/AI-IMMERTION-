# 01 — Empathy Research & User Observation Framework

**Project**: Integrated Retail Store Billing, Real-Time Inventory & Online Ordering Platform  
**Target Entity**: Sri Chamundi Stores & Tea Stall (Tamil Nadu, India)  
**Status**: Framework & Proposed Templates Created — *Pending Real-User Field Data Execution*

---

## 1. Background & Context

**Sri Chamundi Stores & Tea Stall** is a hybrid retail establishment located along a high-traffic highway in Tamil Nadu. The business operates a dual revenue model:
1. **Fresh Tea & Bakery Counter**: Serving highway commuters, truckers, and local patrons with hot tea, coffee, rusks, murukku, and glass-jar packaged cookies.
2. **Retail Grocery Store**: Selling packaged FMCG goods, cooking oils, dairy products (Amul, MilkyMist), staple grains, detergents, and household supplies to surrounding neighborhood residents.

### Operational Context & Challenge
During morning and evening peak hours, the physical store experiences heavy foot traffic at both the tea stall and retail counter. Simultaneously, local neighborhood customers frequently call or visit asking for home deliveries or item availability. Operating without a synchronized digital system causes three distinct human frustrations:
- **Cashiers** face stress handling long billing queues manually while trying to remember prices for hundreds of unbarcoded or packaged items.
- **Store Owners** experience anxiety over unknown inventory stock levels, missing items, and delayed restocking from wholesale suppliers.
- **Customers** experience delay standing in queues, frustration when items requested are out of stock, and lack of transparency regarding online delivery status.

### User Group 1: Store Owner / Manager
- **Demographics**: Small business operator managing finance, procurement, and daily staff supervision.
- **Primary Need**: Real-time visibility into business revenue, exact stock counts, low-stock warnings, and profitability margins without needing complex accounting knowledge.
- **Emotional Driver**: Peace of mind, financial security, avoiding stockouts of high-selling items (e.g., milk, tea dust, popular biscuits).

### User Group 2: Store Cashier / Counter Staff
- **Demographics**: Front-desk retail worker responsible for fast billing, ringing up items, taking cash/UPI payments, and serving tea/snacks.
- **Primary Need**: A simple, ultra-fast billing interface with quick barcode/SKU lookup, clear change calculation, and instant receipt generation.
- **Emotional Driver**: Reducing peak-hour queue stress, avoiding manual calculation errors, and delivering fast customer service.

### User Group 3: Local & Highway Customers
- **Demographics**: 
  - *Local Residents*: Families ordering weekly groceries and fresh staples.
  - *Highway Commuters*: Travelers making quick stops for hot tea, packaged cookies, water bottles, and ice cream.
- **Primary Need**: Clear product availability, accurate pricing, fast checkout, and transparent order tracking when ordering remotely.
- **Emotional Driver**: Convenience, saving time, confidence that ordered goods are actually in stock.

## 3. Empathy Research Methodology

To ensure human-centered design, the empathy research methodology combines **Contextual Inquiry**, **Direct Observation**, and **Semi-Structured Interviews**.

### A. Direct Observation Protocol
Research observers shadow counter staff during two peak windows:
- **Morning Window (7:00 AM – 10:00 AM)**: Peak tea/biscuit sales + morning dairy/staple grocery pickups.
- **Evening Window (5:00 PM – 8:30 PM)**: Peak household grocery shopping + evening tea rush.

**Observation Focus Points**:
1. How long does a single transaction take during peak vs. off-peak hours?
2. How often do cashiers have to ask the owner for a price or check stock manually?
3. How do customers react when an item they came for is out of stock?
4. What manual workarounds (paper pads, calculators, memory) are currently used at the register?

### B. Interview Questions Framework

#### For Store Owner:
1. *“Walk me through your typical day from opening the store to closing.”*
2. *“How do you currently know when it’s time to reorder products from suppliers?”*
3. *“What happens when an item runs out unexpectedly during a busy weekend?”*
4. *“How do you track whether your store made a profit at the end of the day or week?”*
5. *“What is your biggest fear when leaving the staff to manage the store alone?”*

#### For Cashier / Counter Staff:
1. *“What is the hardest part of your job during the 6:00 PM evening rush?”*
2. *“How do you handle pricing for items that don’t have printed barcodes?”*
3. *“What causes customer complaints or arguments at the counter?”*
4. *“If you could change one thing about the billing setup, what would it be?”*

#### For Customers:
1. *“How often do you shop at Sri Chamundi Stores, and what do you usually buy?”*
2. *“Have you ever visited or called the store only to find an item was out of stock?”*
3. *“What makes you choose between buying in person versus ordering for home delivery?”*
4. *“How would you feel if you could check exact item stock on your phone before visiting?”*
