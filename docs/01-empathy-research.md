# 01 — Empathy research and observation plan

**Status: protocol prepared; fieldwork not evidenced.**

The source project is named for Sri Chamundi Stores & Tea Stall and contains store-gallery assets. Descriptions of exact peak hours, staff habits, customer demographics and time spent counting stock were not supported by supplied field records. Treat them as questions to investigate, not facts to repeat.

## Recruitment and consent

Invite an owner/manager, cashier/counter worker and customers who actually use the store. This is a suggested sample for useful coverage, not a claim that participants are already recruited. The assignment's explicit three-person minimum concerns prototype testers; it does not by itself impose three customer interviews plus two staff interviews.

Explain that this is a student project, participation is voluntary, they can skip questions or stop, and their answers are not a staff-performance assessment. Obtain permission separately for notes, audio, photographs and use in a report. Do not publish contact details, signatures, faces or unredacted recordings by default. Use participant codes in public material.

## Non-leading interview prompts

**Owner/manager:** Walk through how you decide what needs restocking. Tell us about the last time the recorded quantity differed from the shelf quantity. What information do you check when you are away? Which task would you most like to make easier? What tools already work well?

**Cashier:** Show how you complete a normal sale. What happens when a price or quantity is unclear? What was difficult in the last busy period? How do you correct a mistake? Which steps must still work when the network or device is unavailable?

**Customer:** Tell us about your most recent purchase here. How did you decide whether the item would be available? What happened while waiting or ordering? How did you know the order was accepted? What would make the process less convenient?

Do not ask participants to agree that a new application is needed. Listen for evidence that a simpler process change would be better.

## Observation protocol

With permission, observe a normal period and a busy period identified by the store, rather than assuming fixed hours. Record the date, duration, setting and task. Separate what was directly visible from interpretation. Do not interfere with transactions or photograph sensitive screens. Measure times only when the start/end event is defined and the participant has agreed.

## Interview record — duplicate per actual participant

- Record ID / participant code / role: **not recorded**
- Actual date, duration, location and interviewer: **not recorded**
- Consent and allowed uses: **not recorded**
- Questions asked and replies: **not recorded**
- Exact quote, with language and any labelled translation: **not recorded**
- Observed actions: **not recorded**
- Researcher's interpretation and uncertainty: **not assessed**
- Contradictory findings or needs the prototype does not address: **not assessed**
- Redacted source file and private original reference: **not recorded**

Populate this record from the real activity. Do not copy the prompts into the answers or turn an AI-generated example into a quote. Existing genuine notes may be transcribed with their actual dates and provenance; do not reconstruct dates or speech from memory as exact records.

## Synthesis

For every supported pain point, record an evidence ID, quote/action, interpretation, affected role and frequency within the observed sample. An issue seen once is one observation, not a percentage of all customers. Update the empathy map, journey map and problem statement after synthesis, including evidence that contradicts the proposed solution.
