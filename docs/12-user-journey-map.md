# 12 — User journey observation worksheet

**Status: draft journey structure; actions and pains below are prompts to investigate, not observed findings.**

| Role and stage | Action to observe | Information the person may need | Possible difficulty to investigate | Evidence ID | Opportunity / later test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer — plan | Decide what to purchase and how to contact/visit the store. | Availability and ordering options. | Uncertainty before a trip or order. | Pending. | Compare a simple availability message with catalogue information. |
| Customer — select | Find an item and choose quantity. | Product identity, price and quantity. | Difficulty interpreting availability or controls. | Pending. | Observe search/cart task. |
| Customer — confirm | Complete an order or counter purchase. | Total and next step. | Unclear payment/confirmation state. | Pending. | Test explanation of confirmation. |
| Customer — follow up | Check progress or correct an issue. | Current stage and contact/recovery option. | Unclear order status. | Pending. | Test tracking and error recovery. |
| Cashier — bill | Identify products, adjust quantities and complete sale. | Correct items, totals and stock response. | Repeated lookup, hesitation or correction steps. | Pending. | Observe billing and print tasks. |
| Owner — review | Inspect stock/transactions and select an action. | Actionable stock and report information. | Hard-to-interpret summaries or duplicate entry. | Pending. | Test low-stock and report tasks. |

During fieldwork replace the proposed actions with the actual sequence. Record thoughts/feelings only from participant reports or label them as interpretations. Include workarounds and successful existing practices. Link every final pain point to an interview or observation, then map the chosen intervention to a validation task.
