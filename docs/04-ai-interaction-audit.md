## Human decision still needed

When a genuine decision is made, record who made it, the actual date, supporting research, alternatives rejected and reasons. Update `data/project-review.json` and link the redacted record. A null human decision is deliberately displayed as pending.

## New-session record form

Session ID; actual date; tool/model as shown by the tool; design question; exact user prompt or clearly labelled paraphrase; original output reference or faithful summary; alternatives; factual corrections; human decision and rationale; resulting change; limitations. Leave unknown fields unrecorded.
